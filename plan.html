Plan

Workshop stages

  1. 01 Before the workshop: Gather perspectives

    Participants’ challenges, examples, conceptual gaps, and research needs, shared in advance of the online sessions.

    If you take part A short survey

  2. 02 During the workshop: Discuss and debate

    Structured discussion of the three topics, from where the watershed falls short to what research should come next.

    If you take part Two 2-hour online sessions

  3. 03 After the workshop: Synthesize and refine

    Draft research priorities, prepared by the organizing team from the survey responses and the workshop discussions.

    If you take part Feedback on the draft priorities

  4. 04 Outcome: Shape a research agenda

    A paper intended for submission to a leading interdisciplinary peer-reviewed journal in hydrology and water research.

    If you take part An invitation to coauthor
